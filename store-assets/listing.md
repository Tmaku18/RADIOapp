## Full description

NetworX Radio is a live station for independent music.

Tune into genre stations, hear what is playing now, and vote with fire when a song hits. Artists upload music for rotation, go live, and reach listeners without a label in the middle.

What you can do:

- Listen to live stations across rap, country, rock, pop, gospel, and more
- Discover artists and songs picked for you
- Vote, favorite, and follow the music you want to hear again
- Message artists and other listeners
- Go live or step into the DJ booth when your account is approved
- Buy songs and manage Pro Radio or Pro Networx inside the app

Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Terms: https://pro-networx.com/terms
Privacy: https://pro-networx.com/privacy

## Google Play Data safety

- Collects: name, email, user IDs, photos, audio, approximate location, purchase history, app interactions
- Shared: with service providers that run the app (authentication, payments, crash reports). Not sold.
- Optional: camera and microphone only when the user goes live or opens the DJ booth
- Users can request account deletion inside the app
- Data is encrypted in transit

## Reviewer notes

Demo listener

- Email: reviewer@networxradio.com (create this account before submission)
- Password: set a one-time password and put it in App Store Connect notes, not in the repo

Path to try

1. Sign in with the demo account. Terms are accepted at sign-up.
2. Open Radio and let a station play. Swipe the artwork to change station.
3. Open Discover, then a profile, and use the menu to Report or Block.
4. Settings includes Restore purchases, Legal, and Delete account.
5. Subscriptions are offered with Terms, Privacy, auto-renew text, and Manage subscription. There is no external checkout link on iOS.

Sign in with Apple and Google are both available. Account deletion is under Settings. Store subscriptions must be cancelled in the store; the delete dialog says so.
