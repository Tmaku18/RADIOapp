## What NETWORX is (memorize this)

**Public description, safe to say now.** NETWORX is a music discovery platform and creative network. Listeners tune into artist-authorized radio stations, hear 30-second samples, buy music directly, and connect with creatives on Pro-Networx.

**What not to add.** NETWORX is not a licensed radio broadcaster, not an official chart reporter, and not a record label. It does not pay radio royalties. It does not promise fame, income, or a set number of listeners.

**Who it is for.**

| Person | In product language | What they came to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listener | Prospector. The role label in signup says Listener. | Hear stations, vote, chat, discover, and buy songs. |
| Artist | Artist. Older copy says Gem. Do not lead with Gem. | Upload, get on rotation, see analytics, sell songs, go live. |
| Creative service provider | Producer on signup. Pro-Networx marketing still says Catalyst. | List services, show work, be found, message. |
| DJ or instrumentalist | DJ, Instrumentalist. | Upload and, if approved, go live. One account can hold more than one maker role. |

**How money works today (say prices only after the supervisor confirms they match the live app and the stores).**

| Offer | Price in the product docs | Careful wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery placement | $1.99 for 1. Packs: 3 for $4.99, 5 for $9.99, 10 for $17.99, 25 for $39.99, 50 for $75.99, 100 for $150. Packs of 3 or more add free placements (1, 2, 4, 10, 20, 40). | Each placement adds about **100 scheduled exposures**. An exposure is a scheduled play, not a promised listener. New artists get 10 welcome placements to split across songs. |
| Pro-Radio | $4.99 a month. First month $1.99 on the website. | On-demand songs and playlists, only for songs whose artists opted in. The free live radio does not become on-demand. |
| Pro-Networx | $9.99 a month. First month $4.99 on the website. | Profiles, services, studios, job board. Resumes and contact info are part of the subscription. Ask the supervisor whether messaging is still free during beta before you print that. |
| Pro Bundle | $12.99 a month. | Pro-Radio and Pro-Networx together. |
| Song and beat sales | Artist picks a fixed price: $0.99, $1.99, $2.99, $4.99, $9.99, $19.99, $29.99, or $49.99. | 30-second sample first. Purchase unlocks full playback and download. Artist balances pay out through Stripe Connect once the account can receive funds. |
| Refinery submission | $4.99 a song, unless a beta switch is on. | Structured review. Staffed toward at least 100 reviews. Not a promise of 100 finished reviews from named people. |
| Livestream tips | $1.99, $4.99, $9.99, or $24.99. | Optional support during a live set. |
| The Yield | $0.10 added for a completed Refinery review. Redeem at $5, $10, or $25. | Earnings for verified review work. Not pay for pressing play. |

**Words to use:** music discovery platform, NETWORX Radio, artist-authorized radio, 30-second sample, direct purchase, discovery placement, community ranking, creative network, Pro-Networx, The Wake, The Refinery, The Yield.

**Words never to use on a flier, caption, email, or school poster:** buy airtime, cheap promotion, get famous fast, exposure only, sign a deal, guaranteed plays, guaranteed listeners, royalties, Billboard, Luminate, SoundExchange, official chart, licensed radio station, record label, get paid to listen.

**The Butterfly Effect.** It is the product name. In public, it means small actions (a vote, a follow, a purchase) are visible on NETWORX. It does not mean a vote will launch a career.

# Part 3. Brand system and claim rules

## 3.1 Master flier spec

Every flier uses this system so the set looks like one company.

**Sizes.**

| Use | Ratio | Pixels or paper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story, reel cover, status | 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 |
| Feed post | 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 |
| Print handout | US Letter | 8.5 × 11 in, and a 4 × 6 in version cut from the same layout |

**Colors (match the app, Dimension Cyber).**

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Void Black | `#050505` | Background |
| Surface | `#0A0A0C` | Panels |
| Neon Cyan | `#00F0FF` | Headlines, buttons, links |
| Signal Pink | `#FF007F` | "Live" or on-air marks only |
| Pulse Yellow | `#F4D03F` | A single number or price, used sparingly |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main text |
| Muted Silver | `#A0A0AB` | Secondary text |

Do not switch this set to the unused campaign palette (purple, blue, green, rose gold). That palette is not the app.

**Type.**

- Headline: Unbounded, or the closest wide gothic the tool has. Short. One idea.
- Body: Inter or Outfit.
- Small labels (ON AIR, the feature name): a mono font, wide letter-spacing, small size.

**Logo.** Use the full NETWORX Radio wordmark (butterfly plus the words). Do not crop the header down to the butterfly alone. The butterfly can also sit as artwork beside the wordmark.

**Layout, every flier.**

1. Wordmark.
2. One headline from the brief. Do not add a second headline.
3. One or two sentences from the brief body. You may tighten them. You may not add a new promise.
4. One screenshot or a phone frame of that feature. If the screenshot is missing, the flier is not done.
5. One call to action and a QR code to that campaign's URL.
6. The line: **By artists, for artists.**

**QR and links.** Each campaign has a slug. Build the link as:

`[url]?utm_source=intern&utm_medium=[flier|instagram|tiktok|print]&utm_campaign=[slug]`

Ask the supervisor for the short link if the long one is ugly on paper. Keep a sheet of the final links.

- A full fan CRM or mailing studio beyond the artist email list
- Storefront view counts, sample-play counts, and purchase conversion rate
- Licensed broadcasting, royalty reporting, and official chart reporting
- The retired name Discover Me. The name is NETWORX.

## 1. Live radio

**Slug:** `live-radio`  
**Audience:** New listeners.  
**Goal:** Get a first listen on a station, without an account if they want to browse as a guest.  
**URL:** `https://www.networxradio.com`

**What is true.** Each station is a shared stream. Everyone tuned to that station hears the same song at the same position. Listeners do not skip the live radio. Full songs on the station are from artists who signed the full-song opt-in. Other people can listen as a guest and sign in when they vote, follow, or chat. The app can keep playing in the background. On iPhone the song can show on the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island, with mute and votes. On Android, stations can show in Android Auto. There is no interrupting audio ad in the radio stream.

**Flier**

- **Headline:** Same song. Same moment.
- **Body:** NETWORX Radio is artist-authorized. Everyone on a station hears the same song at the same time, and the live stream does not skip. You can listen before you make an account.
- **CTA:** Start listening
- **Formats:** 9:16, 4:5, and print
- **Visual:** The player on a real station, showing the song title and the live state. Not a mock playlist with a skip button.
- **Say:** artist-authorized, shared station, no skip on the live radio, guest listening.
- **Do not say:** licensed station, FM radio, royalties, "every song is free in full," a station count you have not counted on screen.

**Captions**

1. Tune into NETWORX Radio. Everyone on the station is on the same song. The live stream does not skip.
2. Independent artists, one shared station. Hear it before you make an account.
3. This is artist-authorized radio, not a playlist you skip through. Start at networxradio.com.

## 2. Fire or pass, ratings, and temperature

**Slug:** `votes`  
**Audience:** Listeners who want a say. Artists who want a split between fan reaction and peer reaction.  
**Goal:** Get a signed-in listener to vote on the song that is playing.  
**URL:** `https://www.networxradio.com`

**What is true.** During a play, a listener can send fire or pass (the pass control in the app is the poop reaction). One vote per play, and it can be changed.
