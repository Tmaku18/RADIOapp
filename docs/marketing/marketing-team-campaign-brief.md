**Three promises.**

1. **Hear it.** Shared radio stations, Discover clips, and 30-second samples.
2. **Back it.** Fire or pass votes, direct song purchases, and optional tips.
3. **Build it.** Artist tools (uploads, placements, The Wake analytics) and the Pro-Networx creative network.

## 2. Claim rules for every asset

**Say.** music discovery platform, artist-authorized radio, shared station, 30-second sample, direct purchase, discovery placement, scheduled exposures, community ranking, weekly Top 7, Yield for finished review work, creative network, Pro-Networx, The Wake, artist email list.

**Never say.** buy airtime, cheap promotion, get famous fast, sign a deal, we are a label, guaranteed plays, guaranteed listeners, "1,000 listeners," royalties, radio royalties, Billboard, Luminate, SoundExchange, official charts, licensed radio station, get paid to listen, unlimited free streaming, "every song plays in full."

**Numbers.**

- A placement adds about **100 scheduled exposures**. Say "scheduled," never "guaranteed."
- Do not invent a user count, a download count, a city count, or a station count. Use a number only if you can point to where it came from.
- Do not show a sales or conversion rate. Those are not tracked yet (HOLD).

**Music in ads.** Use only the artist's 30-second sample or a clip the artist has approved in writing. Never use third-party music. Full songs on the live radio depend on each artist's opt-in, so do not show full-song playback in an ad unless the artist has agreed to it.

**Prices.** Check the price in the app and on the website the same day you publish. Store prices must match the catalog (see decisions 14 and 19 in the business decision log). If the phone and the copy disagree, the phone wins and the copy is fixed before it runs.

**Refinery and Yield.** The Refinery is open to any signed-in user today. Do not say invite-only, raffle, or referral-only (HOLD). Yield is earned from a completed review. Playing the radio does not earn Yield.

**Paid relationships.** Creators and influencers must disclose any payment or free product in the post (#ad or "paid partnership"). No fake testimonials. No income claims from any creator.

## 6. Video concepts

Film real screens and real people. Get written consent before filming anyone. Use only the artist's sample or an approved clip. Captions on every video (most people watch with the sound off). End every video on the same card: wordmark, one CTA, and the link.

### V1. Same Song, Same Moment (30 seconds, listeners)

- **Hook (0 to 2 s):** Two phones in the same room. Both show the same station, same song, same second.
- **Shots:** Friends on a couch. One taps a station. The other taps the same station. A close-up on the song title, then a hand pressing fire.
- **On-screen text:** "Everyone on the station hears the same song." / "The live radio doesn't skip."
- **CTA:** Start listening at networxradio.com.
- **Platforms:** TikTok, Reels, Shorts.

### V2. Fire or Pass (15 seconds, listeners)

- **Hook:** A close-up of a thumb on the fire button.
- **Shots:** The vote animates. The Popular line moves. Cut to the rating from 1 to 10.
- **On-screen text:** "Fire or pass. You decide what rises." / "Popular is listeners. Peer is makers."
- **CTA:** Cast your vote.

### V3. Hear It, Own It (30 seconds, listeners and artists)

- **Hook:** "You can hear 30 seconds for free."
- **Shots:** An artist page. The sample plays. Cut to the buy button. The full song unlocks in the library.
- **On-screen text:** "Up to 30 seconds free." / "Buy it to unlock full playback and download."
- **VO:** "The artist sets the sample. You decide if you want the full song. The artist is paid from that sale."
- **CTA:** Hear a sample.

### V4. Upload to Rotation (45 seconds, artists)

- **Hook:** "Your song, on a station, in 3 steps."
- **Shots:** Upload a file (test file). Choose the sample window (up to 30 seconds). Open the placement screen for one song. Cut to The Wake.
- **On-screen text:** "A placement adds about 100 scheduled plays." / "Scheduled, not guaranteed."
- **VO:** "Upload. Set your sample. Add a placement for one song. Then watch who heard it."
- **CTA:** Start your artist page.

### V5. See Who Heard It (30 seconds, artists)

- **Hook:** A number ticks up on the analytics screen.
- **Shots:** The Wake. Listens, ears reached, unique listeners, followers. A pointer on each one.
- **On-screen text:** "Ears reached counts each person once." / "Unique listeners counts every time."
- **CTA:** Open your analytics.

### V6. Show the Work (30 seconds, creatives)

- **Hook:** A portfolio image fills the screen.
- **Shots:** The Pro profile. Services. The studio map (approximate). A job post. A guest opens the public link on a phone with no account.
- **On-screen text:** "One link for your work." / "Guests can open your profile."
- **CTA:** Join Pro-Networx.

### V7. Live Room (30 seconds, listeners and DJs)

- **Hook:** "They are live right now."
- **Shots:** A live session card. The watch screen. Chat scrolls. A tip button appears (optional).
- **On-screen text:** "Approved artists and DJs go live." / "Chat. Tip if you want."
- **CTA:** See who is live.
- **Rule:** Film only a session that is actually live or a recorded one. Never fake the live badge.

### V8. Listen Anywhere (15 seconds, listeners)

- **Hook:** The phone is locked. The Dynamic Island shows the song.
- **Shots:** Mute from the lock screen. A vote from the lock screen. Cut to a car with Android Auto showing a station list.
- **On-screen text:** "Vote from your lock screen." / "Stations in Android Auto."
- **CTA:** Get the app.

### V9. Keep the Credit (15 seconds, listeners and artists)

- **Hook:** A song on the radio. A tap on record.
- **Shots:** Record a clip. The artist-credit sticker goes on. Post as a 24-hour story. Send to a friend.
- **On-screen text:** "Leave the credit on." / "Stories last 24 hours."
- **CTA:** Make a clip.

### V10. Built by Artists (60 seconds, brand, 4 AM Signal)

- **Hook:** A founder speaking directly to the camera, in a studio, late at night.
- **Shots:** The founder explains why the product was built. Short cuts of real artists (with consent) using the app. A shot of the radio playing.
- **VO:** Founder's own words, no scripted income or fame claims.
- **On-screen text:** "Independent artists. Real radio. Direct sales." / "By artists, for artists."
- **CTA:** Start listening.
- **Rule:** Founder and artist footage needs written consent. No outcome promises in the founder's words.

### V11. Hear it. Vote it. Own it. (6-second bumper, all platforms)

- Three cuts: the station, the fire button, the buy button. Logo at the end.

## 9. Compliance guardrails

- **Rights.** Only artist-approved sample or clip audio in ads and posts. No third-party music.
- **Disclosure.** Paid or free-product creator posts carry a clear disclosure (#ad or "paid partnership").
- **Testimonials.** Use only real, consented quotes. No invented reviews, no income claims.
- **Age.** The app is rated Teen (13+). Adults only for purchase, placement, and subscription ads.
- **Store rules.** Digital goods on iPhone use Apple's billing. Do not route iPhone users to a web purchase page without legal approval. Store copy and ads must match the store listing.
- **Privacy.** Never export fans' email addresses from an artist's list. No screenshots of private messages, phone numbers, payout amounts, or identity documents.
- **Trademarks.** No bidding on competitor brand names. No competitor logos.
- **Legal and public pages.** Link to the terms, privacy, DMCA, and community guidelines pages when a campaign asks for a sign-up or upload.
