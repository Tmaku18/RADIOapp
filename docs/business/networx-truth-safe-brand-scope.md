## 1. The Core Truth

The name is not changing. NETWORX is the app, the brand, the platform, the music marketplace, the radio, the creative network, and the long-term home for live discovery.

The scope separates the company into truthful product lanes so the team can build aggressively without advertising anything that is not active yet.

**Core rule.** NETWORX can be ambitious in private planning, but public marketing must be literal: advertise what exists now, label future capabilities as roadmap, and never imply official licensing, royalty reporting, or chart reporting until those systems are active and approved.

**Truth-safe positioning statement.** NETWORX is a music discovery platform and creative network where independent artists, creatives, and service providers get heard, get seen, connect, sell, and grow.

**Longer positioning statement.** NETWORX gives underground artists and creatives a place to be discovered: artist-authorized radio stations, 30-second samples, direct song sales, profiles, and a creative services network in PRO-NETWORX. Fans vote on what they hear, follow artists, and support them directly. Licensed broadcasting and official reporting are future phases.

## 2. NETWORX Product Lanes

### Lane 1: NETWORX Music Marketplace (live)

Artists directly authorize NETWORX to sample, promote, sell, and deliver their music.

- 30-second samples on artist pages, Discover, and Liked Songs
- Sample window chosen by the artist at upload (5–30 seconds, half-second precision); admins can adjust it
- Artist profiles and song pages with share links (`/open/artists/:id`)
- Full-track purchase unlocks full playback and download
- Purchased songs (and your own uploads) in My Library
- Liked Songs are sample-only saves; buying the song unlocks the full version
- Stripe checkout on the web; App Store and Google Play billing in the iOS and Android apps
- Fixed song price ladder; NETWORX keeps a platform fee and the artist receives the rest through Stripe Connect payouts
- Artist analytics: plays, listens, ears reached, unique listeners, likes, and followers

### Lane 2: NETWORX Radio (live, artist-authorized)

- 30 genre/region stations of non-interactive, live radio-style programming
- Full songs play only when the artist signs the **Full-Song Radio Opt-In** at upload (a non-exclusive license from the artist to stream that song on NETWORX Radio and in DJ programming)
- Listeners vote 🔥 / 💩, rate songs 1–10, comment per song, and chat live; votes feed the song's Temperature
- Admin DJ Booth for live DJ programming; artist livestreams with tips
- Discovery placements: artists buy placements per song; each placement is 100 rotation exposures (an exposure is a scheduled play, not a guaranteed listener)
- **Pro-Radio** subscription: on-demand playlists of songs whose artists opted in to Pro-Radio streaming

### Lane 3: PRO-NETWORX (live, unchanged in purpose)

The creative services, networking, and marketplace side of NETWORX, at [pro-networx.com](https://www.pro-networx.com).

- Profiles for producers, engineers, photographers, videographers, designers, stylists, DJs, instrumentalists, managers, and other creatives, with featured work and portfolios
- Service listings, beats, studios, and a job board
- Search and discovery by category and location where available
- Messaging, inquiries, and booking interest through DMs and service messages
- Shareable public profiles (`/pro-networx/u/:id`) that guests can open
- PRO-NETWORX subscription for premium access

### Lane 4: NETWORX Social Layer (live)

Marketed as community features, never as official radio or chart activity.

- User and artist profiles, follows, likes, favorites, comments, and shares
- Feed posts (audio, video, images), song stories, and radio clips
- Direct messages with message requests
- Discover feed of artists and creatives
- Competition and spotlight features

## 4. Claims Matrix: Replace Risky Language

| Avoid / do not claim yet | Use this instead | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| licensed radio station | artist-authorized radio / music discovery radio | Songs play under licenses granted directly by the artists who opt in. Broadcast/performance licensing is a future level. |
| official chart reporting | building toward official reporting as a future roadmap item | Chart reporting requires separate approval and infrastructure. |
| radio royalties | direct sales and marketplace earnings | Current artist revenue comes from song sales, tips, and Refinery review pay, not radio royalty reporting. |
| every song plays in full / unlimited free streaming | full songs on NETWORX Radio from artists who opted in; 30-second samples elsewhere | Full plays depend on each artist's opt-in. On-demand full playback requires a purchase or Pro-Radio. |
| guaranteed fame or success | built to help artists gain visibility and support | Avoid outcome guarantees. |
| Billboard / Luminate / SoundExchange reporting | not active; possible future roadmap | The Full-Song Radio Opt-In states it does not promise chart or SoundExchange reporting. |
| 1,000 guaranteed listeners / guaranteed plays to listeners | discovery placements with measurable exposures and analytics | A placement is 100 scheduled exposures; who is tuned in at that moment is not guaranteed. |
| buy airtime | discovery placement | Avoids radio royalty and broadcast confusion. |
| preview-to-purchase conversion analytics | plays, listens, ears reached, unique listeners, likes, followers | Conversion, storefront views, and sample-play counts are not tracked yet. |

## 5. Brand Mission, Promise & Belief

**Mission.** NETWORX exists to uncover hidden talent and help artists, creatives, and service providers turn attention into opportunity.

**Belief.** Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not. NETWORX exists to help more talent reach the people who are looking for it.

**Current promise.** NETWORX gives independent artists and creatives a platform to present their work, get heard on radio, connect with people, build community, and earn directly. NETWORX does not promise fame, guaranteed income, official chart credit, or radio royalties.

**Internal doctrine.** NETWORX is not here to promise fame. NETWORX is here to create opportunity.

## 6. Audience

**Primary listener.** Wants to find underground talent early. Curious, culture-aware, tired of repetitive algorithms, interested in hidden gems, and willing to vote for and support artists directly.

**Primary artist.** An independent creator who wants more visibility and direct monetization. Actively creating, trying to grow, tired of being buried, looking for fans, and open to selling directly.

**Primary creative service provider.** A creative worker or freelancer who wants visibility, networking, and paid opportunities. Needs a profile, a marketplace presence, a way to show work, and a place to be found.

## 7. Voice & Tone

**Voice.** A confident cultural operator: clear, direct, ambitious, artist-first, community-focused, business-minded, premium but not corporate, exciting but not misleading.

**Tone rules**

- Speak with energy, but do not overpromise.
- Use "built to help," "designed to support," "created for," "artist-authorized," "marketplace," "network," and "future roadmap."
- Avoid guarantees unless they are technically measurable, contractually true, and operationally deliverable.
- Separate what is active now from what is planned next.

**Preferred vocabulary:** music discovery platform, NETWORX Radio, artist-authorized radio, creative networking platform, PRO-NETWORX, artist marketplace, 30-second samples, direct track purchases, discovery placements, artist pages, creator profiles, service listings, support artists directly, future licensed live discovery infrastructure.

**Clean legal/advertising statement.** NETWORX does not operate as an official chart reporter or as a fully licensed internet radio broadcaster. NETWORX Radio streams songs under licenses that artists grant directly when they opt in, alongside a marketplace where creators authorize NETWORX to sample, promote, sell, and deliver their work. Broader licensed live discovery infrastructure is a future phase.
