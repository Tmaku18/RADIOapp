# Changelog — November 2026

**Mobile release:** 1.0.99 (133)  
**Focus:** Store launch readiness, radio clips and song stories, lock screen and Dynamic Island controls, guest browsing, account security, backend cost and scale

---

## Store launch readiness

- **Launch requirements:** Report and block, signup terms acceptance, paywall disclosures, iOS privacy manifest, crash reporting, bottom player navigation, and store listing assets; the listing matches the shipped app and drops the advertising ID (migrations `133_terms_accepted_at`, `134_apple_authorization_code`).
- **Now Playing everywhere:** Current song on the iPhone home screen widget and lock screen, an Android home widget, and a station list in Android Auto.
- **App links:** Shared links open in the installed app.

## Growth tools

- **Discovery and search:** Discover ranks by engagement, and listeners search the full catalog.
- **Artist tools:** Weekly artist recap, notification quiet hours, a listening tools screen, and an email list listeners join from an artist's page (migrations `135_phase2_growth`, `136_artist_fans`).
- **Payouts:** Waiting song-sale balances pay out once an artist's Stripe Connect account can receive funds.

## Radio player (mobile + web)

- **Controls:** Equalizer bars follow the music; the mute control shows Mute or Unmute to match the sound; a listener's mute holds through song changes and station switches; the app reopens the last station.
- **Layout:** Album cover behind the player tilts under a finger; lyrics scroll freely with the current line highlighted; reactions sit beside the title; song stories sit under the progress bar; lighter glass on the player and bottom bars.
- **Voting:** Fire shows a flame aura and poop a tomato splat on the album; fire responds on first tap; the web mini player shows fire and poop.
- **Gestures:** A drag opens the player on the song already playing; a downward swipe closes it.
- **Lock screen and Dynamic Island:** One cover with a mute control, fire or pass votes from the lock screen, and iPhone Dynamic Island mute and vote buttons; the lock-screen song updates as soon as the track changes.
- **Feedback:** Report a radio song with a reason, rate it 1–10, and open a more menu (migration `143_song_listener_ratings`).
- **Comments:** Each song has a Comments thread beside station Live Chat that returns when the song plays again (migration `144_chat_message_kind`).
- **Audio focus:** Live radio mutes while a clip, video, or Refinery song plays; DJs hear the station in headphones while live.

## Clips, stories, and sharing

- **Record and share:** Listeners record the song on air; Pro-Radio subscribers record any moment of any full song. Clips go to DMs or post as public or private stories, and private stories reach friends (migrations `137_song_stories`, `138_content_privacy_reports`).
- **Clip camera:** Full-screen hold-to-record circle, slide or pinch to zoom, slide-left lock, flip camera; the station stays audible so a take starts on the beat.
- **Editing:** Draggable artist-credit stickers, typed words that drag and pinch, and the song kept in posted videos.
- **Share links:** Station and current artist links, Instagram share for the song on air, artist links at `/open/artists/:id` that load for guests and open the app, and Pro profile sharing by text or DM. Shared website links show the logo.
- **Safety:** People delete or report content.

## Discover, feed, and search

- **Discover:** Cards fly off screen on swipe; Library filters to your own songs; artist results show as glass cards sorted by Popular, Most songs, or Recent, scoped to Everyone (default), Following, or Liked, with a genre filter on Search.
- **Feed:** Tap a video for full screen with cyan controls and swipe it closed; videos pause when you leave the tab; captions are editable.
- **Search as you type:** Live results across radio, Pro-Networx, and admin.

## Accounts and safety

- **Guest browsing:** People browse as guests and sign in when they act (messaging included).
- **Sign-in:** First-time Google and Apple sign-ins land on profile setup; each account claims one unique mobile number and confirms a text code on new sign-ins (migration `146_phone_mfa`).
- **Unique names:** Display names and usernames stay unique once taken (migration `149_unique_display_names`).
- **Account deletion:** Found in Help and at the bottom of the profile, confirmed by typing "delete," and held for 48 hours so signing in again restores the account (migration `148_account_deletion_grace`).
- **Abuse flags:** Bursts of comments or likes are dropped and listed on the admin users page for review (migration `146_activity_flag_detail`).
- **Scores and roles:** Radio scores split into popular and peer votes, and creators can hold more than one maker role (migration `145_user_roles_vote_cohorts`).

## Analytics and placements

- **Metrics:** Ears reached counts unique people; analytics songs sort by plays, views, likes, name, and recency (migration `139_unique_listeners`).
- **Home stats:** Artists see their own stats with platform totals in a second bar, loaded on the first visit.
- **Discovery placements:** Packs deliver 100 exposures each, carry discounted prices and bonus free placements, and every Buy placements tile shows the new pricing.

## Pro-Networx

- Creative cards open the creator's profile; portfolios appear on the creator's own posts; Pro profiles list the artist's radio catalog.
- Pro and Radio share one site, so switching apps skips the sign-in screen.
- Pro menu sits flush on the bottom player bar; profile social links stay with the sticky services rail.

## Design

- **Mobile typefaces:** Satoshi, DM Sans, and Space Grotesk (the website keeps its fonts).
- **Visuals:** Moving butterflies behind Home, translucent visualizer bars, 25% grey panels on Home, and a refreshed lore scene with a prospector striking the gem; theme text stays readable on light pages, dark heroes, and admin tables.
- **Tour:** Updated for the current player, clips, analytics, and radio button locations.

## Backend, performance, and cost

- **Shared radio state:** Station lock and last-good song live in Redis across servers; a jingle plays when the queue is empty; Redis connects over IPv6.
- **Queues:** Push notifications and artist recaps run on BullMQ; the next radio track prefetches before it starts; remaining request bodies are validated.
- **Cost:** Idle stations keep advancing while skipping plays, decision logs, temperature refresh, and credit charges until someone listens; clients poll less often; new media can mirror to Cloudflare R2 once credentials are set (migration `147_prune_play_decision_log`).
- **Speed:** Faster artist profiles, My Songs, notification badge, and public stats (migrations `141_plays_song_stats_index`, `142_load_speed_indexes`).

## Admin

- Queue a song next, drag to reorder, and search songs before adding them.
