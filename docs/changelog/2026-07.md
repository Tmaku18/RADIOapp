# Changelog — July 2026

**Mobile release:** 1.0.21 (44)  
**Focus:** Synced lyrics/captions, radio rights opt-in, Networx Home, Nearby People, Discover video creation, push/app updates

## Lyrics & captions

- **Artist lyrics upload:** Optional plain-text lyrics on web + mobile upload; backend force-aligns to audio (ElevenLabs Forced Alignment).
- **Auto-transcription fallback:** Approved tracks without lyrics can be transcribed (ElevenLabs Scribe); stored with `auto_generated` flag.
- **Players:** Synced lyrics panels on web and mobile; admin **Lyrics** studio; artist edit; listener Library read-only player with seek.
- **Env:** `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` (Railway backend). Optional `ELEVENLABS_STT_MODEL_ID` (default `scribe_v1`).
- **Migrations:** `song_lyrics`, `song_lyrics_alignment`, `song_lyrics_auto_generated`, `get_artist_daily_likes`.

## Full-Song Radio Opt-In

- **Legal:** New page `/full-song-radio-opt-in` (Legal Center → Features & programs); Artist Agreement §5 links to the addendum.
- **Upload UI (web + mobile):** Required radio authorization checkbox; separate DJ livestream checkbox; optional archived DJ mixes checkbox.
- **DB / API:** `songs.opt_in_full_song_radio`, `opt_in_dj_livestreams`, `opt_in_dj_archived_mixes`; create requires radio opt-in.

## Mobile product surfaces

- **Networx Home** as post-sign-in landing; Radio tab remains the full player.
- **Gem dashboard** for artist tools / studio shortcuts.
- **Nearby People:** `users.city` / `zip_code`, geocoding, map pins, directory lists, GPS prompt.
- **Discover:** Reshuffle, artist search, liked/disliked library; **create-video** to liked clips (front selfie camera, countdown); feed storage limit **75MB**.
- **Nav:** Nearby People primary; Room renamed **The Chat Room**; notifications inbox + bell restored.
- **Push:** `user_device_tokens`, expanded alerts, first-login permission prompt; `app_releases` for update prompts.

## Backend / infra

- **Migrations applied (Jul):** device tokens + app releases, users city/zip, feed bucket 75MB, artist daily likes (plus earlier lyrics / radio opt-in).
- **Railway MCP:** Use Cursor `user-railway` tools (`whoami`, `list_projects`, `list_services`, `list_deployments`, `get_logs`, `deploy`, `set_variables`, …).

## Late July (Jul 21–31)

**Mobile releases:** 1.0.22 (45) → 1.0.56 (83)  
**Focus:** Store billing on mobile, feed media uploads, livestream/DJ Booth audio, favorites, iOS push

### Payments and store billing

- **In-app purchases on mobile:** Pro-Networx, tips, credits, and song plays use App Store / Google Play billing with backend verify and grant; Stripe checkout serves the web (migration `109_pro_network_store_billing`).
- **Song and beat sales on mobile:** Bought as store consumables on a fixed price-tier ladder; ownership stays in `song_purchases`, and store sales enter the admin payout queue (migrations `117_song_price_tiers`, `118_song_purchases_store_billing`).

### Discover and feed

- **Create-video:** Mirrored selfie preview and recording, 5s countdown, preview and re-record; camera now starts after the countdown and uploads succeed.
- **Feed posts:** Videos up to 5 minutes, media up to 1GB, gallery video picking, and audio posts with optional cover art. Videos sit in the same card frame as pictures.
- **Upload reliability:** Feed media goes straight to storage with real progress, stalled uploads fail fast with the actual error, and the server re-checks media length.
- **Favorites:** Starred songs live in `song_favorites` (radio-only alerts) and fire likes stay separate; Discover Library holds Favorites, and Liked / Favorites / Library songs open the artist profile (migration `111_song_favorites`).
- **Discover Artists:** Shows artists from liked songs, loads more artists, and handles missing display names.
- **Audio focus:** Live radio pauses while Discover clips, sample previews, or audible feed media play (web + mobile).

### Livestream and DJ Booth

- **Playback:** WHIP streams play via WHEP, fixing black video and silent audio; stuck "starting" sessions no longer block the stream.
- **DJ Booth:** Mobile DJ Booth and Live Performances listing match web; mic talk-over works on iOS, Android, and web; camera toggles on after an audio-only start.
- **Viewers:** Radio pauses while watching a livestream; stream chat removals sync to every viewer.

### Accounts, profiles, and notifications

- **Roles:** Signup roles are honored so Artists and Producers can upload right away.
- **Favorite artists:** Follow favorites with radio-only alerts; artists stop getting a notification after every play.
- **iOS push:** APNs delivery fixed so alerts reach iPhones as well as Android.
- **Sessions:** Mobile sessions stay signed in after backgrounding, and radio votes work on resume; Sign in with Apple works in the browser.
- **Profiles:** Artists upload Pro-Networx cover images and crop covers from Radio Profile; Pro profiles gain a Follow button.
- **Privacy:** Nearby People and artist maps show a general vicinity with approximate city pins.

### Radio, player, and UI

- **Sync:** Radio holds its place on weak connections without jumping backwards.
- **Web listen:** Radio header and track titles display in full; synced lyrics scroll inside their panel.
- **Mobile theme:** Light mode matches the web Daylight Studio theme; the bottom radio bar is more transparent.
- **Pro-Networx (mobile):** Side menu with Discover and Projects, matching web.
- **Stats:** Analytics shows new followers; the home fire tile shows likes.
- **Fixes:** Beat Marketplace loads again; chat senders see their own messages and emoji reactions work at the top of The Room; the tutorial explains how songs and beats differ.
