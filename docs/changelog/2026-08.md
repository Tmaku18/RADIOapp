# Changelog — August 2026

**Mobile release:** 1.0.87 (115)  
**Focus:** Pro-Radio and playlists, radio sync hardening, beta access, per-song placements, Studios, artwork-first redesign, copyright detection

---

## Pro-Radio and playlists

- **Pro-Radio subscription:** On-demand full-song playback from artist pages and the library through a dedicated queue; mobile `/pro-radio` hub, web hub, checkout, and paywall cards (migrations `119_pro_radio_subscriptions`, `120_song_opt_in_pro_radio`).
- **Playlists:** "+ playlist" button on radio, artist, Discover, and library; open a playlist to manage or remove tracks and play it in a full player (migration `121_user_playlists`).
- **Nav:** Pro-Radio sits directly under Radio on mobile, matching the web sidebar.

## Beta access

- **All features unlocked during beta:** `BETA_ALL_FREE` opens every subscription feature; Pro-Networx messaging is free during beta while the price stays visible.
- **TestFlight invites:** Admin can email a beta invite to every user.

## Radio sync and playback

- **Track advances:** Identity-guarded advances and server-driven rotation keep every listener on the scheduled song; radio stays on the current song without replaying or rewinding.
- **Station switches:** A switch holds even when the previous station's update arrives late; a single player instance drives each station.
- **Outros:** Songs play through to the end with an 8s broadcast grace and a 15s boundary handoff.
- **Audio pipeline:** Lossless uploads are transcoded to MP3; realtime broadcasts run on Node 22; `station_events` publish to Supabase Realtime (migration `130_realtime_station_events`).
- **Start station:** Radio opens on Ready Now, and New School Rap listeners stay on their chosen station.

## Artists, placements, and analytics

- **Albums:** Artists group uploads into albums on their page (migration `123_albums`).
- **Placements per song:** The credit bank and Allocate screen are retired; artists buy Discovery placements for each song.
- **Welcome plays:** Artists and producers split their 10 signup Discovery placements across songs, and the creator tour explains the gift (migrations `127`, `128`, `132`).
- **Refinery:** Artists add songs to The Refinery; reviewers answer required website questions after picking a song and can seek within it.
- **Roles:** Listeners become artists automatically when they upload or save a Pro profile.
- **Analytics:** Real Listens, a song/profile picker, "plays" replaces "spins," and My Songs reach reads as ears reached.

## Studios and Pro-Networx

- **Studios:** Producers publish studio pages with rate cards, photo uploads, hours, members, and exact or approximate map pins (migrations `125_studios`, `126_studio_hours_members`).
- **Pro-Networx Studios:** Nearby is now part of Pro-Networx Studios, with the map as a Nearby tab and a Radio tab on studio pages.
- **Pro-Networx Home:** Discover Creatives with type filters.
- **Location privacy:** Map pins use ZIP codes and stay offset from real addresses.

## Messaging and social

- **Message requests:** Instagram-style DM requests, Message buttons on profiles, DM menu tabs, and push alerts for new requests (migration `124_dm_message_requests`).
- **Invites:** Listeners invite friends by text, email, or the share sheet.
- **Chat:** Emoji reactions save correctly; DM and radio chat share The Chat Room background, with an animated butterfly backdrop when a song has no art.

## Design and navigation (mobile)

- **Artwork-first redesign:** UI built around album art with electric cyan as the brand accent; full-bleed sign-in; Welcome to the Networx banner on Home; butterfly default cover for songs without artwork.
- **Navigation:** Library moves into Pro-Networx, Upload into My Songs, Rewards and Analytics into More, and Help & Legal gets its own More tab; The Chat Room joins the primary list.
- **Discover swipe:** Icon actions with play between like and dislike, plus moving cover art.
- **Tour:** Refreshed for Pro-Radio, Nearby ZIP, and beta access.

## Web

- **Parity with mobile:** Beats Marketplace, global Chat Room, Discover playlist and library actions, and aligned Pro-Networx and Studios tabs.

## Admin and copyright

- **Copyright:** Flagged songs show badges and a filter; bulk copyright scan for uploads; ACRCloud matches appear in the app and are named in uploader rejection notices so admins can approve rights holders.
- **Moderation:** Shadow ban and restore; confirmation required before a lifetime ban or account deletion on mobile.
- **Tools:** Queue station dropdown, Swipe clip playback and editing, grant or revoke streamer access, and a faster dashboard first paint.

## Payments, security, and reliability

- **Purchases:** Store purchases are kept when the backend call fails; Stripe webhooks fulfill web purchases; App Store refunds revoke entitlements; forced radio skips leave paid credits intact.
- **Security:** Row Level Security on client-exposed tables (migration `129_enable_rls_public_tables`); payment idempotency indexes (migration `131`).
- **API failover:** The mobile app calls Railway first and fails over between hosts; only GET requests retry on 502/503/504 so writes apply once.
